# About Me
ATM I’m really interested in collecting The Book of Mormon, Hedwig and the Angry Inch, and Falsettos!!
While I do wish to have a collection with hundreds of shows, unfortunately I have limited storage at the moment, so all the files I’m gathering are things I am really excited about watching and having.
So, I might not have exactly what you’re looking for, but take a look around! Maybe you’ll find something to enjoy, I really hope you do!
Mostly using Mega, Transfer.it or Google Drive. My links usually last for 7 days, but you can always tell me if you need more time :)

# Rules
- Please send your whole list, even if it’s small.
- I don’t do “this for that” trades, and don’t take rips from sites like YouTube, Archive.org, or other streaming sites.
- Whoever initiates a trade sends their links first.
- Please don't share my links or post the videos anywhere online.
- My trade ratios are usually the standard:
1:1 video | 1:1 audio | 2:1 audio to video
But I'm always open to figuring something out if it makes sense :)
